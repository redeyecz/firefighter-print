## License Types Explained

### MIT License

- ✅ **Permissive**: Very few restrictions
- ✅ **Commercial Use**: Allowed without restrictions
- ✅ **Modification**: Can modify and redistribute
- ✅ **Private Use**: Can use privately
- ⚠️ **Requirement**: Must include copyright notice and license text
- ✅ **Public Sector**: Perfect for government/fire department use

### Apache 2.0 License

- ✅ **Permissive**: Similar to MIT but with patent grant
- ✅ **Commercial Use**: Allowed without restrictions
- ✅ **Patent Grant**: Explicit patent protection
- ⚠️ **Requirement**: Must include copyright, patent, trademark, and attribution notices
- ✅ **Public Sector**: Excellent for government use

### Public Domain (SQLite)

- ✅ **No License Required**: Completely free
- ✅ **No Restrictions**: Use for any purpose
- ✅ **No Attribution Required**: Optional
- ✅ **Public Sector**: Ideal for government use

## Compliance Requirements

To comply with the MIT and Apache 2.0 licenses used by our dependencies:

1. **Include License Text**: Keep a `LICENSES` folder with copies of all dependency licenses
2. **Copyright Notices**: Include copyright notices in distributed software
3. **Attribution**: Provide attribution in documentation or about pages

These requirements are minimal and standard for open source software.
